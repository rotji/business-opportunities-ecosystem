- Distinguished ERP (internal operations), BI (analysis of business data), and external intelligence (the wider business environment).
- Identified the possibility of independent intelligence services that connect to multiple business systems rather than depending on one ERP vendor.
- Broadened the project from ERP to a Business Technology & Intelligence Ecosystem opportunity map.
- Established the market-first, partnership-first, build-last philosophy.
- No product, market entry, or software build decision has been made.
