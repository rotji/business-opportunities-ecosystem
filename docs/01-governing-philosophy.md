# Governing Philosophy: Market-First, Build-Last

## Principle

Never build a product simply because a problem exists. First discover who is already solving it and whether we can create value by partnering, implementing, integrating, distributing, reselling, or supplying a complementary service.

## Decision sequence

1. Discover established providers and emerging startups.
2. Understand their products, customers, business models, distribution, and limitations.
3. Validate the problem with real customers and evidence.
4. Prefer partnership with providers whose solutions already address the need.
5. Explore integration or complementary services where there is a substantiated gap.
6. Monitor startups and new entrants that may be addressing that gap.
7. Build only when a genuine underserved need is supported by a concrete customer request, commercial incentive, or compelling strategic reason.

## Important safeguards

- An apparent gap is not automatically a business opportunity.
- Confirm that the problem matters to customers and that they can and will adopt or pay for a solution.
- Do not assume that a problem is unsolved because it is unfamiliar to us.
- Check whether established vendors or emerging startups are already addressing it.
- Continue monitoring the market even after identifying a promising gap.

## Commercial options before building

Partnership, distribution, implementation, consulting, integration, training, support, and complementary intelligence services may create value without developing a competing platform.

## Operating rule

Exploration is not a commitment to build. A market category is an area to understand, not a product specification.
