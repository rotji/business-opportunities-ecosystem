# Business Technology & Intelligence Ecosystem Map

This is a working map of opportunity areas and the structure for practical exploration. It is not an exhaustive or mutually exclusive industry taxonomy. Companies may operate across several categories.

## Ten exploration categories

### 1. Business Management Systems
Systems businesses use to manage internal operations:
- ERP
- CRM
- HRM
- POS
- Accounting, inventory, procurement and workflow management
- Other operational business applications

### 2. Business Intelligence
Tools and services that turn business data into analysis and decision support:
- Reporting and dashboards
- Performance analytics
- Forecasting and scenario analysis
- Operational and financial analysis
- Decision support

### 3. External Business Intelligence
Tools and services that help businesses understand developments outside their own operations:
- Competitor and pricing intelligence
- Market and industry intelligence
- Supplier and distributor intelligence
- Regulatory and policy intelligence
- Geographic and location intelligence
- Economic and wider environmental signals

### 4. Data Infrastructure
Technology and services that make data available, usable, connected and trustworthy:
- Data platforms and storage
- APIs and data services
- Data integration and interoperability
- Data marketplaces and licensing
- Data quality, enrichment, governance and verification

### 5. Automation and AI
Capabilities that automate or augment business work:
- Workflow and process automation
- AI assistants and agents
- Intelligent document and information processing
- Orchestration and decision automation

### 6. Payments and Financial Infrastructure
Services that enable businesses to accept, move and manage money:
- Payment gateways and processing
- Merchant services
- Financial APIs and embedded finance
- Transaction and reconciliation infrastructure

### 7. Logistics and Supply Chain Infrastructure
Services that move, store, fulfil and coordinate goods:
- Delivery and fulfilment
- Warehousing and distribution
- Shipment tracking and visibility
- Supply-chain coordination and logistics software

### 8. Cloud and Computing Infrastructure
Technology that provides computing and application foundations:
- Cloud computing and hosting
- Storage and databases
- Developer infrastructure and deployment
- Managed cloud and infrastructure services

### 9. Communication and Customer Engagement
Services businesses use to communicate with customers and coordinate interactions:
- Business messaging, email, SMS and voice
- Customer support and contact-centre platforms
- Customer engagement and communication APIs
- Collaboration and notification services

### 10. Identity, Security and Trust Infrastructure
Services that help establish identity, control access and protect systems and transactions:
- Authentication and access management
- Cybersecurity
- Identity and business verification
- Fraud prevention and risk controls
- Trust and compliance-related services

## Important distinction: internal BI and external intelligence

Business Intelligence (Category 2) primarily analyses information about the organisation and its performance. External Business Intelligence (Category 3) examines information about the environment in which the organisation operates. They are distinct but can complement one another.

## Practical exploration rule

The working plan is to select **one representative platform per category**, investigate it properly, and only search for an alternative if the first candidate is unsuitable or a specific comparison is justified. We do not need to examine many near-identical platforms by default.

Prioritise candidates with permanent free plans, recurring free credits or tokens, usable public demos, sandboxes or documentation. Short time-limited trials are not preferred at this early stage and should not be activated without a specific reason.

See [Ten-Category Platform Exploration Plan](05-ten-category-platform-exploration-plan.md) for selection criteria, practical investigation steps and the proposed order.

## Cross-cutting commercial participation

Across all categories, investigate whether value can be created through:
- Partnerships
- Distribution and resale
- Implementation and configuration
- Consulting and adoption support
- Integration
- Complementary services and intelligence
- Selective product development, only when justified

## Classification note

Classify a company by the capability or customer problem being studied, not by forcing the entire company into one box. Record important overlaps. Revise the map when practical research supports a better structure.
