# Business Technology & Intelligence Ecosystem Map

This is a working map of opportunity areas. It is not an exhaustive or mutually exclusive industry taxonomy.

## 1. Business management systems
- ERP
- CRM
- HRM
- POS
- Supply Chain Management
- Accounting, procurement, inventory, workflow management

## 2. Business intelligence
- Reporting and dashboards
- Performance analytics
- Forecasting and scenario analysis
- Operational and financial analysis
- Decision support

## 3. External business ecosystem intelligence
- Competitor and pricing intelligence
- Market and industry intelligence
- Supplier and distributor intelligence
- Regulatory and policy intelligence
- Geographic and location intelligence
- Economic and environmental signals

## 4. Data infrastructure
- Data platforms
- APIs and data services
- Integration and interoperability
- Data marketplaces and licensing
- Data quality, enrichment, and verification

## 5. Automation and AI
- Workflow automation
- AI assistants and agents
- Document and information processing
- Orchestration and decision automation

## 6. Business-enabling infrastructure
- Payments and financial infrastructure
- Logistics and fulfilment
- Cloud and hosting
- Identity, security, and access management
- Communications and collaboration

## Cross-cutting commercial participation
- Partnerships
- Distribution and resale
- Implementation and configuration
- Consulting and adoption support
- Integration
- Complementary services and intelligence
- Selective product development

## Classification note

A company may operate in several categories. Classify by the capability or customer problem being studied, rather than forcing every company into one box. Revise the map when research supports a better structure.
